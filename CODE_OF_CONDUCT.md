# Code of conduct

Lookover follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), in every space of this project: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

In short: be respectful and kind, assume good faith, accept feedback on your work, and leave out harassment, insults and personal attacks of any kind. The Covenant's full text says what is expected, what isn't accepted, and how a breach is handled.

## Reporting

To report a breach, write to the maintainer, Samuel Asensi ([@sasensi](https://github.com/sasensi) on GitHub), privately, or use the repository's [private reporting](https://github.com/lookover-dev/lookover/security/advisories/new). Reports are read promptly and kept confidential. You can also use GitHub's **Report content** on any comment.

## Enforcement

The maintainer applies the Covenant's enforcement guidelines, from a correction to a permanent ban, in proportion to the breach, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break it.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
